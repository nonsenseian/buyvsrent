      Methodology & Disclosures

      Taxes & Income: Uses simplified standard federal tax brackets and state estimates to calculate take-home pay. 401k limits adhere to $24k (Single) or $48k (Joint) caps, growing annually by the user's inflation rate.

      Net Worth Balance Sheet: Assumes Uninvested "Leftover Cash" is deposited into a 0% checking account. Sunk costs (Rent, Property Tax, Maintenance, Interest, HOA, Insurance, PMI at a rate of 0.5% of the home cost) are deducted from Total Net Worth. Principal payments shift cash to home equity.

      Inflation vs Nominal: Graphs display nominal values. Tooltip hover states dynamically calculate Real (inflation-adjusted) purchasing power discounting backwards from the month shown.
